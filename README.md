# Hi there! I'm Margarita Cantero ✨🦅
### 🌸 About Me: Where Science Meets Data

I am a **Data Analyst & Scientist** with a native background in biological sciences and ecology. I love combining the rigorous, curious mind of a researcher with the computational power of data analytics to solve complex business problems, automate workflows, and reveal impactful insights. 

Think of me as a hybrid professional: I bring the analytical depth required to handle messy, real-world scientific data, a sharp corporate mindset focused on strategic metrics, and a bright, authentic energy that makes collaboration a breeze! 💖

* 🎯 **My Current Focus:** Building clean data pipelines, uncovering hidden patterns, and designing interactive dashboards that tell a story.
* 🧠 **Core Areas of Interest:** Complex Statistical Modeling (GLM), Bioinformatics, Predictive Analytics, and Business Intelligence (BI).
* ⚡ **Fun Fact:** I am just as comfortable untangling massive, chaotic scientific datasets in Python using fuzzy matching as I am translating heavy ecological indices into clear business KPIs. I bring a touch of sparkle and a lot of science to everything I code! Ordo Ab Chao ✨
